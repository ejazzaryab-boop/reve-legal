GET HELP WITH REVÉ

Revé is a personal tracking app for logging compound and peptide protocols alongside your menstrual cycle, so you can see your own patterns over time.

Contact us

For any question, bug report, account issue, or billing question, email zareef@usereve.com. We aim to respond within two business days.

If you are writing about a problem in the app, it helps to include your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Account and data

You can export your data or permanently delete your account at any time from the Account section of Settings inside the app. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Subscriptions

Revé subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account. To view, change, or cancel a subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.

Important

Revé is a personal logging tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about any decision regarding your health.
