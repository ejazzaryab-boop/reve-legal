Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trendset LLC ("Revé," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Revé mobile application (the "App").

Revé is a personal tracking app that lets you log compound and peptide protocols and tag them to your menstrual cycle so you can see your own patterns over time. Because the App handles sensitive health information, we've tried to keep this policy clear and specific. If anything here is unclear, contact us at zareef@usereve.com.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Trendset LLC, a limited liability company organized in New York, USA. You can reach us with any privacy question at zareef@usereve.com.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information needed to run the App and provide its features. Specifically:

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and name, and information from the sign-in method you choose (Sign in with Apple or Google). We use Apple and Google only to verify who you are; we do not receive your Apple or Google password.

Cycle information. The period start dates you enter, and the cycle phases the App calculates from them.

Protocol and dose logs. The compounds and protocols you choose to track, the doses you log, and the timing of those doses.

Journal entries. Any check-in notes, moods, or symptoms you choose to record.

Measurements. Body measurements you enter, such as weight over time.

Progress photos. Any photos you choose to upload. These are stored privately and are only accessible to your account.

Health data (HealthKit). If you grant permission, the App can read certain health data from Apple Health to save you from re-entering it. Access is read-only. We never write to Apple Health, and we do not receive your data from Apple Health unless you grant permission. You can revoke this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Basic technical information. Standard information needed to operate a mobile app and diagnose problems, such as app version and general device type.

We do not collect your precise location, and we do not use tracking technologies to follow you across other apps or websites.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Provide the App's core features — logging, cycle-phase tagging, and showing you your own patterns over time.
  • Generate personalized insights about your own logged data.
  • Maintain your account, sync your data across your sessions, and keep your data secure.
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or for any advertising or marketing profiling.

4. How your information is shared

We share information only with the service providers that make the App work, and only to the extent needed to provide the service:

  • Supabase — our backend provider, which hosts your account, your logged data, and your uploaded photos on our behalf.
  • RevenueCat - our subscription management provider, which receives your account identifier and subscription status to manage your access.
  • Apple and Google — used only for sign-in verification.
  • Apple App Store — if you purchase a subscription, payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the App. If ownership of the App changes, your information may transfer as part of that change, subject to this policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active.

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (in the Account section of Settings). Account deletion is permanent and immediate. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, cycle data, protocol and dose logs, journal entries, measurements, and progress photos, and we remove your account itself. There is no recovery period, and deleted data cannot be restored.

You can also export a copy of your data from within the App before deleting.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls on our backend. Your uploaded photos are stored privately and are accessible only to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit who and what can access it.

7. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • Access and export your data from within the App.
  • Delete your account and all associated data from within the App.
  • Control HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data (such as the right to access, correct, or delete it). To exercise any such right, contact us at zareef@usereve.com.

8. Sensitive health information

Revé handles information about menstrual cycles and personal health, which is sensitive. We treat it that way. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers described above that are necessary to operate the App. You remain in control and can delete this data at any time.

9. Children

Revé is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Where your data is processed

Your data may be processed and stored on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located outside your country of residence. By using the App, you understand that your information may be processed in those locations.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
